# Communications

Draft each message for the user to send through their own channels. Staticbot does not send them. Fill every bracket; drop sentences that do not apply. Keep a status page or channel reachable that does not depend on the app's domain.

**Advance notice (3–7 days before):**
We're moving [app] to new infrastructure on [date], from [start] to [expected end, timezone]. Please save your work before [start]. During this time the app will be unavailable. Your usual address stays the same. [Sign-in change, e.g. "Afterwards you'll sign in with a code sent to your email."] Updates: [status URL]. Help: [support contact].

**Reminder (1 hour before):** [App] goes into maintenance at [start, timezone]. Please save your work now. Expected back by [end].

**Maintenance page / status:** [App] is temporarily unavailable while we move it. Next update: [time, timezone]. Updates: [status URL]. Help: [support contact].

**Delay:** The move needs additional checks. [App] remains unavailable. Next update: [time, timezone]. [Known impact]. Help: [support contact].

**Completion:** [App] is available again at [domain]. [Sign-in instructions]. If anything is missing or not working, contact [support].

**Rolled back:** We postponed the move. [App] is available again as before at [domain]. Nothing you saved before [start] was lost. We'll announce a new date.

Native Base44 migrations change sign-in to email code or magic link. Say so in the advance notice and the completion message.
